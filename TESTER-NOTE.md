# A note for YOU's beta testers

*Ready to copy into a message, email or group chat. Change anything you like.*

---

Hi lovely,

Thank you for being one of the first to step into **YOU, Your Own Universe**.

YOU is a quiet place to grow into who you already are. You plant a few habits, goals and dreams each week, tend them one small step at a time, and write down what's on your mind. There are no streaks to keep and nothing to catch up on.

**Getting started (about 5 minutes)**

1. Open **yourownuniverse.life** on your phone.
2. Add it to your home screen so it opens like an app:
   - **iPhone:** in Safari, tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**.
   - **Android:** in Chrome, tap **⋮**, then **Install app**.
3. Tap **Create my account**, then confirm your email. Check spam if it doesn't arrive in a few minutes.
4. Follow the short welcome. It's six pages, and you can skip it.

**Finding your way**

- The **bar at the bottom** takes you to Home, YOUrney, **+ Add**, YOUrnal and YOU.
- The **☰ menu** at the top left has everything else.
- Lost? Open the menu and tap **Find your way**. It's a map of the whole app with four things to try first.

**What I'd love you to try this week**

- Plant your first seeds: choose up to three things to grow.
- Write a few lines in your YOUrnal.
- Choose one value that matters to you, under YOU → Values.
- Try a practice from Home.

**Tell me everything**

Open the menu, tap **Send feedback**, and choose *Something's not right*, *An idea* or *Something I love*. It records which screen you were on, so just say what happened. No note is too small, and the confusing bits are the most useful.

**Good to know**

- What you write is private to you. AI reflections stay off unless you switch them on.
- You can download or delete your data at any time in the menu, under **Your Own Universe**.
- It's a beta, so some things will change and a few may wobble. Pages marked *Coming soon* are still being built.

Thank you for helping shape this. It means a lot.

Cassidy
