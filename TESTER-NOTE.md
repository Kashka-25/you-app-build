# A note for YOU's beta testers

*Ready to copy into a message, email or group chat. Change anything you like. Updated 4 Nov 2026 for the simple beta.*

---

Hi lovely,

Thank you for being one of the first to step into **YOU, Your Own Universe**.

YOU is a quiet place to grow into who you already are. You choose a few habits, goals and dreams, tend them one small step at a time, write down what's on your mind, and keep the moments that matter. Over time it shows you the story of your life as it unfolds. There are no streaks to keep and nothing to catch up on.

**Getting started (about 5 minutes)**

1. Open **yourownuniverse.life** on your phone.
2. Add it to your home screen so it opens like an app:
   - **iPhone:** in Safari, tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**.
   - **Android:** in Chrome, tap **⋮**, then **Install app**.
3. Tap **Step into Your Own Universe**, create your account, then confirm your email. Check spam if it doesn't arrive in a few minutes.
4. Follow the short welcome. You can skip it.

**Finding your way**

YOU has five places, all along the **bar at the bottom**:

- **Home**: how you're arriving today, and what's on for today.
- **YOUrnal**: write, speak or scan a page from your paper journal.
- **+**: add anything, from anywhere.
- **PursYOUts**: your habits, goals, dreams and ideas, and your week (Sow and Harvest).
- **YOU**: your values, and the parts of your life growing from what you do.

**YOUrney** (your memories, chapters and seasons) is in the **☰ menu** at the top left.
Tap **your picture** (top right) for Settings: **Find your way** (a map of everything), **What's new**, **Extras** (optional parts like Focus or YOUniversity, all off to start), colours and your data.

**What I'd love you to try this week**

- Plant one or two things in **PursYOUts**, and tick a habit off for a few days.
- Write a few lines in your **YOUrnal**.
- Add a memory with a photo in **YOUrney**.
- Choose a value or two that matter to you, on **YOU**.
- On Sunday or Monday, **Sow** your week: pick up to three things to tend.

**Tell me everything**

Open the **☰ menu** and tap **Send feedback**, then choose *Something's not right*, *An idea* or *Something I love*. It records which screen you were on, so just say what happened. No note is too small, and the confusing bits are the most useful.

**Good to know**

- What you write is private to you. AI reflections stay off unless you switch them on (Settings → Your data), and they always say what they read.
- YOU works without a signal and catches up when you're back online.
- You can download or delete everything at any time in Settings → Your data. Deleting removes all of it.
- It's a beta, so some things will change and a few may wobble.

Thank you for helping shape this. It means a lot.

Cassidy
