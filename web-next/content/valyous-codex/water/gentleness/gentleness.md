## Essence
Strength that doesn't need to be hard.

## Light
Gentleness is a river smoothing stone: soft, unhurried, and still changing everything it touches. It's a calm voice in a tense moment, a careful hand, words chosen so they can be heard. Gentleness isn't weakness; it takes real strength to stay soft when it would be easier to harden.

## Shadow (excess)
Too much, and gentleness can become avoidance. You might soften every truth until it disappears, or let others walk over you rather than risk seeming harsh. Not every moment needs a soft touch; some need a firm one.

## Void (deficiency)
Without gentleness, life gets sharp-edged. You may be hard on yourself in ways you'd never be with a friend, or brace against the world as if softness would cost you. Harshness often began as protection.

## What keeps it whole
Honesty gives gentleness its spine. A truth told gently is still a truth, and often the one most able to land. Together they make a kindness that can be trusted.

## Questionnaire notes
- What truth have you softened until it no longer says anything?
- Where are you harder on yourself than you'd ever be on someone you love?
