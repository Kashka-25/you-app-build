## Essence
Letting what is be here, so you can meet it with open eyes.

## Light
Acceptance is the moment you stop wrestling the wave and learn to float. It doesn't mean you like what's happening, or that you'll never change it. In practice, it means acknowledging reality as it is right now, including your own feelings and limits. From that honest ground, wise action becomes possible.

## Shadow (excess)
Too much, and acceptance slides into resignation. "That's just how it is" can become a reason never to try, never to speak up, never to leave. Floating turns into drifting somewhere you never chose.

## Void (deficiency)
Without acceptance, life becomes a long argument with what already is. Energy pours into resisting, blaming, or wishing things were otherwise. Beneath the struggle there's often grief waiting for permission to be felt.

## What keeps it whole
Courage is acceptance's companion: accept what cannot be changed, and find the nerve to change what can. Together they know the difference.

## Questionnaire notes
- What are you still arguing with that has already happened?
- Where has accepting things become a way of not asking for more?
