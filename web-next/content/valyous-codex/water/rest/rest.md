## Essence
The sacred art of doing nothing, fully.

## Light
Rest is letting yourself be restored without needing to earn it first. Real rest refills something: it can look like sleep, a slow walk, an afternoon with no plans. From rest, the rest of life becomes possible again.

## Shadow (excess)
Too much, and rest can become a place to hide. Stillness slides into avoidance, recovery into numbing, and the things that would bring you alive keep getting postponed. Some tiredness asks for rest; some asks for change.

## Void (deficiency)
When rest is missing, everything gets thinner: patience, joy, attention, health. You may feel you have to justify every pause. Running on empty is often a story about worth, the belief that you're only allowed to stop once everything is done.

## What keeps it whole
Discipline gives rest its shape, and rest gives discipline its softness. Rest held within a rhythm is neither laziness nor escape. It is the tide going out so it can come back in.

## Questionnaire notes
- What does your tiredness tend to be asking for: sleep, stillness, or something to change?
- Who taught you that rest has to be earned?
