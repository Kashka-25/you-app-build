## Essence
The quiet voice beneath the noise that already knows.

## Light
Intuition is knowing that arrives before the reasons do, like a compass needle settling before you've read the map. It speaks through the body, a tightening or an easing, and through images, dreams and hunches. Put simply, it is your whole self noticing patterns faster than your thinking mind can explain. Listening to it can bring you home to yourself.

## Shadow (excess)
Followed without question, intuition can mistake fear for foresight or a wish for a sign. You might read meaning into everything, or dismiss facts that don't fit the feeling. Not every whisper is wisdom.

## Void (deficiency)
When intuition is ignored, life can feel like following other people's maps. You may second-guess every choice and look outside yourself for permission. That inner voice often went quiet because it wasn't welcome once. It returns when it's listened to.

## What keeps it whole
Clarity walks with intuition, checking the hunch against what's real. The gut suggests; the clear mind considers. Together they make choices you can trust.

## Questionnaire notes
- When has your gut known something long before your mind would admit it?
- How do you tell the difference between intuition and fear?
