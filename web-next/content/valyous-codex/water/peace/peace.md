## Essence
A settled heart, even when life is loud.

## Light
Peace is the still water deep in a lake while wind ruffles the surface. It isn't the absence of trouble; it's a steadiness underneath it. In everyday life, peace looks like responding instead of reacting, choosing calm where you can, and helping the people around you feel safe enough to settle too.

## Shadow (excess)
Too much, and peace becomes peace at any price. Keeping things calm can mean swallowing what needs saying, avoiding a conflict that would lead somewhere better, or numbing out rather than feeling. A quiet room isn't always a peaceful one.

## Void (deficiency)
Without peace, the mind rarely rests. Everything feels urgent and small things land like big ones. You may find yourself bracing even in safe moments, a sign your body learned long ago that calm couldn't be trusted.

## What keeps it whole
Courage keeps peace real. Sometimes the road to lasting peace runs straight through a hard conversation. Peace gives courage its steadiness; courage stops peace from becoming silence.

## Questionnaire notes
- What are you keeping quiet about, to keep the peace?
- Where in your body do you feel unsettled most often, and what might it need?
