## Essence
Like water, finding a way around the stone.

## Light
Adaptability is the grace of changing shape without losing yourself. Water fits whatever vessel it's poured into and still stays water. In everyday terms, it's adjusting your plans, habits or views when life shifts, rather than breaking against it. It lets you meet change as a doorway rather than a threat.

## Shadow (excess)
Too much, and you become whatever each room needs you to be. Constant bending can leave you unsure what you actually want, think or believe. A river with no banks becomes a flood, or a swamp.

## Void (deficiency)
Without adaptability, life feels brittle. Every change lands like a crack, and plans become cages you defend. Rigidity often grows from times when change meant danger. Small, safe experiments in flexibility can soften it.

## What keeps it whole
Integrity is the riverbed that adaptability flows through. You can change your course and keep your values. The water moves; the source stays true.

## Questionnaire notes
- When do you bend to fit in, and when do you bend to grow?
- What change are you bracing against right now, and what might it be offering?
