## Close calls, for your eye
- **Peace / Serenity, Security / Safety:** kept one of each; the other can
  be a synonym.
- **Power, Desire, Assertiveness:** chosen on purpose. They're the "shadow"
  qualities The Sovereign Empath invites people to reclaim.
- **Alchemy and Sovereignty:** YOU's own language (the Alchemist, the
  Sovereign Empath). Worth having as values people can hold.
- **Not included:** Loyalty and Justice (they already find a home as
  sub-values of Commitment and Fairness), Purpose and Connection (Pillar
  names), Awakening (a tier name).
