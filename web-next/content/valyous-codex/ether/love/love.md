## Essence
The warm light that makes everything grow.

## Light
Love is the sun on a garden, warmth that helps life unfold. It's care, delight and deep connection, given and received. In plain terms: wanting the good of another, and yourself, and acting on it with kindness, affection and presence. Love takes many forms: romantic, familial, friendship, love of place, love of life.

## Shadow (excess)
Too much, and love can tighten into possession, dependency or losing yourself in another. "I love you" can become "I need you to be what I want." The sun that never sets scorches the garden.

## Void (deficiency)
Without love, life grows cold and lonely. You might feel unlovable, or keep love at arm's length to stay safe. The longing for love is itself a sign you're made for it. Love often begins again in small ways: a kind word, a gentle hand, a moment of self-care.

## What keeps it whole
Freedom keeps love healthy. Love that lets the other be themselves, and you be you, grows deeper and truer. Hold close, hold open.

## Questionnaire notes
- Where is love easiest for you to give, and hardest to receive?
- Where does love become a need to control?
