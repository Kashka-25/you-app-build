## Essence
Letting your outside match the light within.

## Light
Authenticity is being the same shape inside and out, like a tree that grows the way its seed intended. It's living from your own values and voice rather than borrowed ones. Plainly: expressing who you truly are, and making choices that fit that truth, even when it's not what's expected. It lets others meet the real you.

## Shadow (excess)
Too much, and "being myself" can become a reason not to grow, or not to consider others. Identity hardens into a fixed story. The truest self is still becoming.

## Void (deficiency)
Without authenticity, life can feel like a costume. You might play roles to be liked, safe or successful, and feel strangely absent from your own life. The mask usually went on for good reasons. Taking it off is gentle, gradual work.

## What keeps it whole
Adaptability lets authenticity breathe. You can flex to meet the moment and still stay true. Being real includes growing.
