## Essence
Seeing the world as if for the first time.

## Light
Wonder is looking up at the night sky and feeling both tiny and part of everything. It's the gasp at a sunrise, the child's "whoa" at a beetle. In plain terms: a sense of amazement and awe at life's mystery and beauty. Wonder reminds you that the world is bigger than your worries.

## Shadow (excess)
Too much, and wonder can drift into escapism: always gazing at the stars and never at the ground. Life's practical needs can slip away while you're lost in the marvel.

## Void (deficiency)
Without wonder, life turns ordinary and flat. Everything seems known and explained. Busy-ness and worry are often wonder's quiet thieves. Slowing down, even for a minute outside, can let it back in.

## What keeps it whole
Clarity keeps wonder grounded. You can marvel at mystery and still see clearly. Awe and understanding are old friends.

## Questionnaire notes
- When did something last take your breath away?
- What have you stopped noticing because you think you've seen it before?
