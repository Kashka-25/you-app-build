## Essence
Leaning back into life, believing you'll be caught.

## Light
Trust is the bridge you walk across without testing every plank. It's believing in yourself, in others, or in life's unfolding enough to let go of control. Plainly: relying on someone or something with an open heart, and on your own ability to handle what comes. Trust makes closeness, risk and rest possible.

## Shadow (excess)
Too much, and trust becomes naivety. You might ignore warning signs, or give your heart to people who haven't earned it. Blind trust can lead you onto broken bridges.

## Void (deficiency)
Without trust, life becomes guarded and exhausting. You check, control and brace for disappointment. Distrust is usually learned for good reasons. Small experiences of safety can slowly teach the heart that some bridges hold.

## What keeps it whole
Wisdom guides trust. Trust thoughtfully, test gently, and let trust grow as it's earned. Open-hearted and clear-eyed, both.

## Questionnaire notes
- Who or what has earned your trust, and how do you know?
- What would you do differently if you trusted yourself a little more?
