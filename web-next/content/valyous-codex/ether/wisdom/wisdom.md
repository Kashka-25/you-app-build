## Essence
Knowledge that has been lived, and softened by time.

## Light
Wisdom is the old tree that has weathered many seasons. It knows when to bend, when to hold, when to speak and when to stay quiet. Literally: using knowledge, experience and understanding to make good judgements, especially in complex situations. Wisdom comes from living, reflecting and learning from both joy and pain.

## Shadow (excess)
Too much, and wisdom can become aloofness, or the belief that you always know best. Advice flows but listening stops. The old tree can forget it was once a sapling.

## Void (deficiency)
Without wisdom, life can feel like repeating the same lessons. You might act on impulse or others' opinions rather than your own understanding. Wisdom grows from reflection: pausing to ask what life has been teaching you.

## What keeps it whole
Humour keeps wisdom humble. Taking yourself lightly, even as you think deeply, is its own kind of wisdom. The wisest people laugh easily.

## Questionnaire notes
- What has life taught you that you could only have learned the hard way?
- When does your wisdom stop listening?
