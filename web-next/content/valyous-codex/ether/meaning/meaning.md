## Essence
The golden thread that ties your days together.

## Light
Meaning is the star you steer by, the sense that your life is part of something that matters. It turns ordinary moments into chapters of a larger story. Put simply: knowing why you do what you do, and feeling connected to purposes beyond yourself. Meaning can be found in love, work, faith, creativity or service.

## Shadow (excess)
Too much, and the search for meaning can weigh everything down. Every choice must be significant, every day purposeful. The simple joys get lost beneath the need for depth.

## Void (deficiency)
Without meaning, life can feel like going through motions. You might ask "what's the point?" more often than you'd like. That question is itself a sign of a soul reaching for something. Meaning often reappears in small, honest acts of love.

## What keeps it whole
Playfulness lightens meaning. Not everything has to matter enormously. Sometimes the most meaningful thing is a moment of simple, pointless joy.

## Questionnaire notes
- When have you felt most sure your life mattered?
- Where does the search for meaning make ordinary joy feel not enough?
