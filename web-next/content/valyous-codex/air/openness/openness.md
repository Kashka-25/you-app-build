## Essence
An open window: letting new air in.

## Light
Openness is the door left ajar for the unexpected guest. It welcomes new ideas, people and experiences without needing to control them. In plain terms: being willing to consider other views, try new things and let yourself be changed. Openness keeps life fresh and relationships alive.

## Shadow (excess)
Too much, and openness becomes a door with no frame. You might take in every opinion and lose your own, or let in what harms you. An open mind still needs a centre.

## Void (deficiency)
Without openness, life narrows to the familiar. New ideas feel threatening and differences dangerous. Closing up often follows hurt. Opening again, a crack at a time, is a brave and gentle practice.

## What keeps it whole
Boundaries give openness a frame. You can welcome the world and still choose what stays. The window opens; the house remains.

## Questionnaire notes
- What idea or person have you closed the door on too quickly?
- Where has being open meant letting in something you didn't want?
