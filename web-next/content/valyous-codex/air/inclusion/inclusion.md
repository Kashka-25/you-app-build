## Essence
Making room for everyone at the table.

## Light
Inclusion is a table with extra chairs, always ready for one more. It's making sure people of every background, ability, identity and story feel they belong. Inclusion means noticing who's missing and widening the circle.

## Shadow (excess)
Too much, and inclusion can lose discernment, welcoming every voice equally, even the ones that cause harm. It can also become performative: saying the right words without making real change.

## Void (deficiency)
Without inclusion, some people are left outside, unheard and unseen. You may not notice who's missing, or feel left out yourself. Being excluded hurts; being included can heal.

## What keeps it whole
Wisdom guides inclusion. Knowing how to keep a space safe lets more people truly belong in it. Together they make a circle that is wide and kind.

## Questionnaire notes
- Where are your welcoming words not yet matched by action?
- Who is missing from the circles you're part of?
