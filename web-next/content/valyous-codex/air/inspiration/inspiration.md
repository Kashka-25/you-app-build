## Essence
Breathing life into ideas.

## Light
Inspiration is a gust of wind filling a sail. It's the sudden spark of an idea, the lift of being moved by something beautiful, and the energy to create. Inspired people inspire others: enthusiasm is contagious.

## Shadow (excess)
Too much, and inspiration can become waiting for the muse: only acting when you feel inspired, so many ideas never become real. Inspiration without action is a beautiful daydream.

## Void (deficiency)
Without inspiration, life can feel flat and uncreative. You may feel stuck or uninspired, waiting for something to move you. Inspiration often finds us when we're already moving.

## What keeps it whole
Discipline turns inspiration into creation. Show up, and inspiration has somewhere to land. Together they make ideas real.

## Questionnaire notes
- What are you waiting to feel inspired about before you begin?
- What moves you most, and when did you last make time for it?
