## Essence
Letting the truth breathe, kindly.

## Light
Honesty is clear air between people: nothing hidden in the haze. It's saying what's real, even when it's uncomfortable, and being truthful with yourself first. Plainly: not deceiving others or yourself, and being willing to speak and hear the truth. Honesty makes trust possible, and trust makes closeness possible.

## Shadow (excess)
Too much, and honesty becomes a blade. Truth delivered without care can wound, and "I'm just being honest" can excuse cruelty. Not every thought needs to be spoken, and not every moment is the right one.

## Void (deficiency)
Without honesty, small untruths gather like fog. You might tell people what they want to hear, or avoid facing what you know inside. Dishonesty usually starts as protection. Speaking one small truth can clear more air than you'd expect.

## What keeps it whole
Compassion softens honesty's edge. Truth told with care is heard; truth thrown is defended against. Kind honesty is the most useful kind.

## Questionnaire notes
- What's one truth you've been avoiding telling yourself?
- When has your honesty been more about you than about the other person?
