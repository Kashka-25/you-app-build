## Essence
Passing on what you've learned.

## Light
Mentorship is an older tree sheltering saplings until they're strong. It's sharing your knowledge, experience and encouragement to help someone else grow, and being humble enough to learn from those ahead of you too. Mentors help others find their own path.

## Shadow (excess)
Too much, and mentorship can become control or ego: needing to be the expert, or shaping someone in your image instead of helping them become themselves.

## Void (deficiency)
Without mentorship, people learn everything the hard way. You may feel you have nothing to teach, or have no one to learn from. Everyone knows something someone else needs.

## What keeps it whole
Openness keeps mentorship two-way. The best mentors keep learning from those they guide. Together they grow both people.

## Questionnaire notes
- Are you helping someone become themselves, or more like you?
- Who could you learn from, or who could learn from you?
