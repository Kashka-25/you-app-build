## Essence
The breeze that lifts the weight, even for a moment.

## Light
Humour is a window thrown open in a stuffy room. It lets in air, perspective and connection. Plainly: the ability to see and share what's funny, including in yourself and in hard times. Laughter can be medicine for the spirit and a bridge between people.

## Shadow (excess)
Too much, and humour becomes a shield. Every tender moment gets a joke, every hard feeling a punchline. People laugh with you, but they rarely get close.

## Void (deficiency)
Without humour, life can feel heavy and too serious to breathe. You might take every mistake to heart. Humour often goes quiet in hard seasons, and that's okay. It tends to return with rest and good company.

## What keeps it whole
Vulnerability gives humour depth. Laughing at yourself while letting others see the real you makes humour an embrace rather than a deflection.

## Questionnaire notes
- What do you joke about so you don't have to feel it?
- Who makes you laugh until it hurts, and when did you last see them?
