## Essence
When the fog lifts and you can see the path.

## Light
Clarity is a still pond reflecting the sky. When the water settles, you can see all the way to the bottom. In plain terms: understanding what you think, feel and want, and being able to express it simply. Clarity makes decisions lighter and conversations cleaner.

## Shadow (excess)
Too much, and clarity can harden into false certainty. Everything becomes black and white, and nuance gets lost. Seeing clearly isn't the same as seeing everything.

## Void (deficiency)
Without clarity, life can feel foggy and overwhelming. You might circle the same decisions, unsure what you want. Confusion often means too many voices, or too little quiet. Stillness tends to let the water settle.

## What keeps it whole
Openness keeps clarity humble. Clear sight plus a willingness to be wrong makes for true understanding. Hold your view firmly, and lightly.

## Questionnaire notes
- What do you already know, beneath the confusion?
- Where might your certainty be keeping something new from reaching you?
