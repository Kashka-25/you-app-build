## Essence
The art of being truly heard and truly listening.

## Light
Communication is a bridge built plank by plank between two inner worlds. At its best it's honest and kind at once. In plain terms: saying what's real, listening for what's underneath, and repairing when words go wrong. Good communication makes people feel less alone.

## Shadow (excess)
In excess, communication can become a need to be heard at all costs: over-explaining, filling every silence, winning rather than understanding. Words multiply while connection thins.

## Void (deficiency)
When it goes missing, things are felt but not said, and distance grows in the gaps. Silence can be a habit learned in homes where speaking up wasn't safe. The unspoken is often what most needs air.

## What keeps it whole
Presence steadies communication. Listening fully before answering, and letting silence breathe, turn talk into true meeting.

## Questionnaire notes
- What is the conversation you keep rehearsing in your head but not having?
- When do you talk to be understood, and when to be right?
