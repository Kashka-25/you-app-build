## Essence
Every day, a new window opening.

## Light
Learning is the mind stretching its wings. It's picking up a new skill, understanding something deeper, or seeing an old thing in a new light. Literally: gaining knowledge or ability through study, practice or experience, including the lessons life brings uninvited. A learning life stays young at heart.

## Shadow (excess)
Too much, and learning can become a way of never beginning. One more course, one more book, before you feel ready to act. Knowledge piles up while life waits.

## Void (deficiency)
Without learning, the mind settles into grooves. You might feel stuck, or believe you're "not a learner" because school once made you feel small. Learning is far bigger than school. It returns when it's allowed to be playful and your own.

## What keeps it whole
Wisdom asks what learning is for. Knowledge becomes wisdom when it's lived, tested and shared. Learn, then do, then learn again.

## Questionnaire notes
- What would you love to learn if no one would ever grade you?
- Where does learning more become a way of not starting?
