## Essence
A balanced scale, held with an open hand.

## Light
Fairness is the sense that everyone deserves a seat at the table. It notices when things are uneven and cares about making them right. Put simply: treating people justly, giving and taking in balance, and standing up when someone isn't being treated well. Fairness is how trust grows between people and within communities.

## Shadow (excess)
Too much, and fairness becomes scorekeeping. Every kindness is tallied, every slight remembered. Relationships start to feel like ledgers rather than living things.

## Void (deficiency)
Without fairness, imbalance creeps in: in relationships where one gives and the other takes, or in systems that leave some out. You might tolerate being treated unfairly, or look away when others are. Noticing the imbalance is where repair begins.

## What keeps it whole
Forgiveness keeps fairness warm. Justice without mercy grows cold; mercy without justice grows careless. The scale needs both hands.

## Questionnaire notes
- Where in your life is the balance of giving and receiving uneven?
- When does your sense of fairness turn into keeping score?
