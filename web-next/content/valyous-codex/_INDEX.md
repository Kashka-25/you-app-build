## Open questions for Cassidy
- **Kindness, Generosity, Loyalty, Security** didn't make the 40. Generosity is
  drafted as a sub-value of Gratitude; Kindness is planned under Compassion; Loyalty and
  Security could be subs of Commitment / Stewardship. Promote any that matter.
- **Purpose** and **Connection** are Pillar names, so the values are called
  Meaning and Love/Trust instead, to keep the two systems from blurring.
- **Challenges:** each drafted entry keeps its tier headings empty. The existing
  7 challenges per value still live in `src/constants/values.const.js`; the
  curated 4-per-tier sets are a separate backlog item.
