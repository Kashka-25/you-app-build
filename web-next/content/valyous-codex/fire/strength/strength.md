## Essence
Power you can lean on, in body and in will.

## Light
Strength is an old tree in a storm: it bends, it creaks, it stays. It's the capacity to carry what needs carrying, in your muscles and in your spirit. Strength shows up in lifting, in enduring, and in standing firm for yourself and others when it counts.

## Shadow (excess)
Too much, and strength can become hardness. You may refuse help, hide pain, or believe you always have to be the strong one. Strength that never rests eventually cracks.

## Void (deficiency)
Without strength, life can feel like too much to hold. You might feel fragile, easily knocked over, or dependent on others to carry you. Strength grows back in small, steady ways, through rest, movement and support.

## What keeps it whole
Gentleness keeps strength humane. The strongest hands are often the gentlest. Together they let you hold firm without holding hard.

## Questionnaire notes
- Where do you have to be the strong one, and what does it cost you?
- What would help you feel steadier in your body and spirit?
