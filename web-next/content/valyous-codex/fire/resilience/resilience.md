## Essence
The ember that stays warm through the longest night.

## Light
Resilience is the ember beneath the ash, still glowing after the storm has passed. It isn't about never falling, but about finding your way back up, often changed, sometimes stronger. In plain terms: the capacity to recover from difficulty, adapt, and keep going. It's built slowly, through support, rest and each small return.

## Shadow (excess)
Too much, and resilience hardens into toughing it out alone. You might keep going long after you should have stopped, or refuse help because you "can handle it." Endurance becomes a wall around your need.

## Void (deficiency)
When resilience wears thin, every setback feels like the final one. Hope becomes hard to reach. That's not weakness; it's what happens when too much has been carried for too long. Rest and gentle support are what help the ember catch again.

## What keeps it whole
Vulnerability is what makes resilience humane. Letting others see you struggle, and help you, is how the ember is fed. Strength and softness keep each other alive.

## Questionnaire notes
- What has helped you through before, when you thought you couldn't go on?
- Where are you being strong when you'd rather be held?
