## Essence
Reaching toward the peak you can see from where you stand.

## Light
Ambition is the part of you that looks at the mountain and wants to climb it. It gives direction to effort and shape to dreams. Plainly put: a desire to grow, achieve or contribute beyond where you are now, and the willingness to work for it. Healthy ambition says "I can become more," not "I am not enough."

## Shadow (excess)
Too much, and every summit only reveals a higher one. Worth becomes tied to achievement, and rest feels like falling behind. You might climb past the people you love without noticing.

## Void (deficiency)
Without ambition, life may drift in comfortable circles. You might hold back from goals for fear of failure, or of being seen to want. Often ambition is quiet rather than absent, waiting for permission to want something openly.

## What keeps it whole
Gratitude lets ambition enjoy the view. Wanting more and appreciating what's here can live together. The climb is part of the life, not a waiting room for it.

## Questionnaire notes
- Whose approval is hiding inside your ambitions?
- What would "enough" look like for you, if you let yourself define it?
