## Essence
Letting life be a little bit of a game again.

## Light
Playfulness is the child in you who still believes puddles are for jumping. It's lightness, experimentation, the joy of doing something just because it's fun. In practical terms: approaching life, and people, with openness and enjoyment rather than constant seriousness. Play isn't the opposite of work; it's often what makes the work bearable, and the learning stick.

## Shadow (excess)
Too much, and play becomes a way to never land. Every serious moment gets deflected with a joke, and responsibilities are always tomorrow's. The game continues while the real conversation waits.

## Void (deficiency)
Without play, life grows heavy and grown-up in all the wrong ways. You might feel you have to earn fun, or that silliness is beneath you. The part of you that wants to play hasn't gone; it's waiting to be invited.

## What keeps it whole
Responsibility gives play a safe field to run in. When the important things are cared for, lightness becomes freedom rather than escape.

## Questionnaire notes
- When did you last play with no purpose at all?
- When is your lightness a gift, and when is it a way of not staying?
