## Essence
The fire that makes you forget to check the time.

## Light
Passion is the hearth-fire of the soul, warming everything it touches. It's what you'd do for no reward, what lights you up when you speak of it. Literally: strong, energising care for something, a craft, a cause, a person, that pulls you toward it. Passion turns effort into devotion.

## Shadow (excess)
Too much, and the fire consumes its own fuel. Passion can become obsession, burning through sleep, relationships and health. What once warmed you starts to scorch.

## Void (deficiency)
Without passion, days can feel grey and interchangeable. You do what needs doing, but nothing catches. Often the spark isn't gone, only buried under duty or disappointment. It tends to relight near the things you once loved.

## What keeps it whole
Rest banks the fire so it lasts through the night. Passion that knows when to pause can burn for a lifetime rather than a season.

## Questionnaire notes
- What did you lose track of time doing, the last time you truly lost track of time?
- Where does your passion start to cost more than it gives?
