## Essence
Marking the good moments on purpose.

## Light
Celebration is lanterns strung across a summer night. It's pausing to mark what matters: wins, milestones, love, survival. Celebration turns ordinary days into memories, and reminds us that life is worth gathering for.

## Shadow (excess)
Too much, and celebration can become constant distraction, a party that never ends so nothing ever has to be felt. It can also turn into excess that leaves you depleted rather than lifted.

## Void (deficiency)
Without celebration, achievements slip by unnoticed and life starts to feel like one long to-do list. You might feel uncomfortable being celebrated, or wait for a big enough reason that never comes.

## What keeps it whole
Rest gives celebration its meaning. The pause after the party is where the joy settles in. Together they create a rhythm of effort, celebration and renewal.

## Questionnaire notes
- What feeling might celebrating be helping you avoid?
- What good thing have you let pass without marking it?
