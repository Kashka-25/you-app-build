## Essence
The open sky inside you, where you choose your own direction.

## Light
Freedom is the felt sense that your life belongs to you. Like a bird that knows the sky is open, it isn't about always flying, but knowing you could. In plain terms: the ability to make your own choices, move your own way, and say yes or no from the inside. Freedom lets you shape a life, not just fill one.

## Shadow (excess)
Too much, and freedom becomes an allergy to anything that holds you. Every commitment feels like a cage, so you keep one foot out the door. The bird that never lands never builds a nest.

## Void (deficiency)
Without freedom, life can feel lived on someone else's terms. You might follow rules you never chose and swallow wants you never voiced. The restlessness you feel is a signal, not a flaw: something in you wants room to breathe.

## What keeps it whole
Commitment gives freedom a home to return to. Choosing what to be bound by is itself the deepest freedom. Roots and wings, both.

## Questionnaire notes
- Where do you feel most caged right now, and who holds the key?
- What has your need for freedom cost you, and was it worth it?
