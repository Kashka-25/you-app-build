## Essence
Tending yourself as you'd tend someone you love.

## Light
Self-care is watering your own garden, not just your neighbours'. It's paying attention to what you need, physically, emotionally and mentally, and meeting those needs with kindness. Real self-care is often simple: sleep, food, movement, boundaries, rest, and time that's just yours.

## Shadow (excess)
Too much, and self-care can become self-indulgence or avoidance. Bubble baths can't fix what needs a hard conversation. Self-care can also become another task on the list, done perfectly and without joy.

## Void (deficiency)
Without self-care, you run on empty. You may notice exhaustion, irritability, illness or numbness. Many caregivers put themselves last for so long they forget they matter too.

## What keeps it whole
Service keeps self-care generous. Caring for yourself makes you better able to care for others. Together they make a full cup that overflows.

## Questionnaire notes
- What are you calling self-care that is really avoidance?
- When did you last ask yourself what you need, and listen?
