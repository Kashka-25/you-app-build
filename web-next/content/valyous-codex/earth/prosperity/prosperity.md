## Essence
Enough, and the means to share it.

## Light
Prosperity is a harvest barn filled before winter. It's having the resources you need to live well, care for those you love, and give to others. Healthy prosperity means security, freedom and the ability to choose, not just accumulation.

## Shadow (excess)
Too much, and prosperity can become greed or endless accumulation. Money can turn into a measure of worth, and enough is never enough. Wealth without purpose can isolate.

## Void (deficiency)
Without prosperity, life can feel constrained and anxious. Money worries may shape every decision. Many people carry beliefs about money from childhood that quietly keep them in lack.

## What keeps it whole
Generosity keeps prosperity healthy. Wealth that flows outward stays meaningful. Together they let prosperity be a blessing rather than a burden.

## Questionnaire notes
- How much is enough, and how will you know when you're there?
- What did you learn about money growing up that still shapes you?
