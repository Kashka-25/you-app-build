## Essence
Useful, humble help.

## Light
Service is a bridge built for others to cross. It's using your skills, time and energy to make life better for others, without needing the spotlight. Service can be a career, a volunteer shift, or simply being the person who shows up when help is needed.

## Shadow (excess)
Too much, and service becomes self-sacrifice. You may give until you're empty, feel guilty saying no, or tie your worth to how useful you are. Service that ignores your own needs eventually runs dry.

## Void (deficiency)
Without service, life can feel self-focused and less meaningful. You may feel disconnected from something bigger than yourself. Helping others is one of the surest paths back to purpose.

## What keeps it whole
Self-care keeps service sustainable. You can only pour from a cup that's refilled. Together they make help that lasts.

## Questionnaire notes
- Is your helping ever a way of feeling worthy?
- Where could your gifts be useful to someone else?
