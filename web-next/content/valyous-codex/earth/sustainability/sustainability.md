## Essence
Living so the earth can keep giving.

## Light
Sustainability is planting trees whose shade you may never sit in. It's living in a way the earth, and the people who come after us, can keep living with. Sustainability shows in choices about what we buy, waste, eat, travel and protect.

## Shadow (excess)
Too much, and sustainability can become eco-guilt or judgement: feeling that nothing is ever enough, or looking down on others' choices. Burnout helps no one, including the planet.

## Void (deficiency)
Without sustainability, the future feels far away and consumption easy. You may feel helpless about the scale of the problem, so nothing changes. Small actions matter, especially when shared.

## What keeps it whole
Prosperity balances sustainability. Thriving now and caring for the future can go hand in hand. Together they build a world worth inheriting.

## Questionnaire notes
- Where does guilt about the planet stop you from acting at all?
- What small change would actually fit your life?
