## Essence
Trusting the seed while it's still underground.

## Light
Patience is the farmer's faith: you plant, you tend, and you let the season do its work. It knows that good things often grow slowly and out of sight. In practical terms: staying calm and kind while waiting, whether for results, for people, or for yourself to change. Patience turns time into an ally.

## Shadow (excess)
Too much, and patience becomes endless waiting. You might tolerate what should be challenged, or stay in situations that never change. The seed you wait for may not be in the ground at all.

## Void (deficiency)
Without patience, everything feels urgent and every delay like failure. You might pull up the seedlings to check whether they're growing. Impatience often hides fear, that if it doesn't happen now, it never will.

## What keeps it whole
Courage knows when waiting has become avoiding. Patience holds; courage acts. Together they know when to wait and when to move.

## Questionnaire notes
- What are you trying to rush that might need more time?
- Where has patience turned into putting up with something?
