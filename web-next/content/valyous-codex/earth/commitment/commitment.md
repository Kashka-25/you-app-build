## Essence
Choosing to stay, and meaning it.

## Light
Commitment is putting down roots and letting them grow deep. It's the promise that holds on the ordinary days, not just the easy ones. In practical terms: dedicating yourself to a person, a path or a practice over time, and following through even when enthusiasm fades. Depth only comes to what we stay with.

## Shadow (excess)
Too much, and commitment keeps you rooted in soil that no longer nourishes you. You might stay out of duty, fear or pride, long after something has ended. Loyalty becomes a way of not listening to yourself.

## Void (deficiency)
Without commitment, life can feel like a string of beginnings. Nothing gets old enough to become deep. Hesitating to commit is often protection against loss. Starting small, with one chosen promise, can make staying feel safe.

## What keeps it whole
Freedom keeps commitment alive. A promise freely made, and freely renewed, is stronger than one held by fear. Staying is most meaningful when leaving was possible.

## Questionnaire notes
- What are you committed to that you would choose again today?
- Where does loyalty keep you somewhere you've outgrown?
