## Essence
Tending the body that carries you through every day of your life.

## Light
Health is the soil in which the rest of life grows. When it's cared for, energy, mood and clarity have something to root into. Literally: looking after your body and mind through food, movement, sleep and care, as an act of respect rather than punishment. Health isn't a finish line; it's an ongoing friendship with the body you live in.

## Shadow (excess)
Too much, and health becomes a project of control. Every meal is a test, every rest day a failure, and the body an enemy to be managed. The pursuit of wellness can quietly make you unwell.

## Void (deficiency)
When health is neglected, the body starts to speak louder: tiredness, aches, low mood. Often this isn't laziness but overwhelm, or a long habit of putting yourself last. Small acts of care are often the kindest place to start.

## What keeps it whole
Acceptance keeps health gentle. Your body will change, age and sometimes struggle. Caring for it as it is, not only as you wish it were, is the deepest health of all.

## Questionnaire notes
- Do you care for your body as a friend or manage it as a project?
- What is your body asking for that you keep postponing?
