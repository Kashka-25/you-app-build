## Essence
Knowing where you end and where others begin.

## Light
Boundaries are how you tell the truth about your limits. They protect your energy, your time and your body, and they make real closeness possible, because people can trust your yes when you're able to say no.

## Shadow (excess)
Too rigid, and boundaries become walls. You might keep people out before they can hurt you, or use rules to avoid the risk of real contact. Protection can quietly turn into isolation.

## Void (deficiency)
Without boundaries, you may say yes when you mean no, and resentment builds up in the gaps. You end up living other people's priorities. The exhaustion is information: a part of you asking to be protected.

## What keeps it whole
Openness keeps boundaries alive. A good boundary is a gate, not a wall. It knows when to open, to whom, and how far.

## Questionnaire notes
- Where do you say yes while your body is saying no?
- Which of your boundaries were built for a danger that has since passed?
