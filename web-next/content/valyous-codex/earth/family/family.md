## Essence
The people who knew you before you knew yourself.

## Light
Family, by blood or by choice, is where belonging takes root. It's the people who hold your history, show up in the ordinary and difficult hours, and let you be known across the years. Tending family is tending your roots.

## Shadow (excess)
In excess, family can become enmeshment or obligation. Loyalty to the group may cost you your own voice, and old roles keep you smaller than you've become. Love and duty get tangled.

## Void (deficiency)
When family feels absent or painful, there can be a lasting ache for home. That longing is real and worth honouring. Many people find, or build, the family they need, and it counts just as much.

## What keeps it whole
Boundaries let family be a root rather than a cage. With a clear sense of self, you can belong without disappearing, and love without losing your way.

## Questionnaire notes
- What role were you given in your family, and does it still fit?
- Who is your family now, by any definition that feels true?
